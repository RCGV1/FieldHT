# FieldHT Support

FieldHT is a native iPhone companion for compatible Benshi UV-PRO radios and related accessories.

## Getting started

1. Turn on Bluetooth on your iPhone.
2. On the radio, open Main Menu and toggle Pairing.
3. Open FieldHT and use the Connect screen to discover or reconnect to your radio.

## What FieldHT can do

- Connect to compatible radios over Bluetooth LE
- Manage channels and zones
- Tune VFO with split RX/TX and tone settings
- Import channel plans from CSV, PDF, and text files
- Configure APRS and packet beacon settings
- Track satellite passes with Doppler-aware tuning
- Configure supported programmable buttons and BS22 speaker-mic settings

## Troubleshooting

- If the radio does not appear, make sure pairing mode is enabled on the radio and Bluetooth is available on the phone.
- If a connection drops, reopen the Connect screen and reconnect to the saved radio first.
- Satellite features require location permission, and some satellite/reference features also require network access.
