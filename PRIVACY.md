# FieldHT Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

FieldHT is an iPhone app for controlling compatible handheld radios over Bluetooth.

## What FieldHT uses

- Bluetooth: used to discover, connect to, and control compatible radios and supported speaker microphones.
- Location: used for satellite pass predictions, pointing, and related geolocation features when you choose to use them.
- Network access: used for optional online data sources such as SatNOGS, N2YO, and RepeaterBook.
- Files you import: used only to import channel data you explicitly choose from documents such as CSV, PDF, or text files.

## Data collection

FieldHT does not require an account.

FieldHT does not sell your personal information.

FieldHT stores app preferences and radio-related settings on your device so the app can reconnect to your last radio, remember interface choices, cache satellite/reference data for offline use, and keep your workflow fast.

## Third-party services

When you use optional online features, requests may be sent to:

- SatNOGS
- N2YO
- RepeaterBook

Those services operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

## Your choices

- You can deny Bluetooth or Location access in iOS Settings at any time.
- You can avoid online lookups by not using the related network-backed features.
- You can remove the app to delete locally stored app data from the device.
